# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in **RepoXray** a harassment-free, constructive, and technically rigorous experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy security engineering community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy, technical accuracy, and respect toward other contributors
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code and security review feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the security of downstream users and the open-source ecosystem

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information or live leaked credentials without explicit permission
- Submitting malicious test payloads or intentional supply-chain backdoors

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer via GitHub Security Advisories or repository moderation channels at <https://github.com/Ra9mirez11/repoxray>. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
